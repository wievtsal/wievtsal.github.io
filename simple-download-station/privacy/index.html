Privacy Policy

Simple Download Station · Effective 9 October 2026

Simple Download Station ("the app") is an iPhone and iPad remote control for the Download Station package on your own Synology NAS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: we do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. We have no servers and no accounts.

Information we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not send any information to the developer.

Information stored on your device

To connect to your NAS, the app stores on your device the server address and connection settings, a session token and, if you use two-factor authentication, a device token. If you turn on "Stay signed in", it also stores your DSM username and password. Passwords and tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain. Your favorite download folders, app settings and a cached copy of your subscription status are also stored on your device. None of this is sent to the developer. Signing out removes the stored password and session tokens; the last server address is kept so you can sign in again quickly. Items kept in the iOS Keychain may remain on the device after the app is deleted, until you reinstall the app and sign out, or erase the device.

Network connections

The app connects directly to the NAS address you enter. If you sign in with a QuickConnect ID, the app asks Synology's QuickConnect service (global.quickconnect.to) to find your NAS. That request is handled by Synology under Synology's privacy policy. Links, magnet links and .torrent files you add are sent only to your NAS.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer does not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy is available at apple.com/legal/privacy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
