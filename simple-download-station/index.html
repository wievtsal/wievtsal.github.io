Simple Download Station

An iPhone and iPad remote for Download Station on your Synology NAS. Add magnet links, URLs and .torrent files, and manage downloads at home over your local network or anywhere with QuickConnect.

Support

Questions or problems? Email support.3k@icloud.com.

Requirements

  • A Synology NAS running DSM with the Download Station package installed
  • A DSM account allowed to use Download Station

Subscriptions

Simple Download Station Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
