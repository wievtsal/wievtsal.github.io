How do I connect to my NAS?

Enter your NAS address (IP or hostname) or your QuickConnect ID, then sign in with your DSM account. If your account uses two-factor authentication, enter the code when asked.

Does it work away from home?

Yes. Add a QuickConnect ID and the app uses it whenever your NAS isn't reachable on the local network. With auto-switch on, it picks the right route for you.

Where are my credentials stored?

Only on your device. The app talks directly to your NAS (or through Synology QuickConnect) — there is no third-party server in between. See the Privacy Policy.

Which files and links can I add?

Magnet links, HTTP(S) URLs and .torrent files. Choose the destination folder on your NAS before you start the download.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad → your name → Subscriptions, and choose Simple Download Station. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
